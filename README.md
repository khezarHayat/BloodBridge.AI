# BloodBridge AI

BloodBridge AI is an AI-powered blood donation and emergency blood management platform designed to connect patients, hospitals, blood banks, and verified blood donors through a unified digital system.

The platform aims to simplify emergency blood requests by helping patients find compatible donors and available blood banks based on blood group, location, availability, and urgency.

### Key Features
- **Patient Management:** Register patients and submit emergency blood requests.
- **Donor Management:** Donor registration, profiles, availability, and blood group information.
- **Smart Donor Matching:** Identify compatible donors based on blood group and location.
- **Hospital Management:** Support hospitals in managing and responding to blood requests.
- **Blood Bank Management:** Manage blood bank information and inventory.
- **Emergency Requests:** Track requests and their response status.
- **Notifications:** Keep relevant users informed about request updates.
- **Role-Based Access:** Separate dashboards and permissions for patients, donors, hospitals, and administrators.

### Technology Stack
- Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite
- Backend: Python, FastAPI
- Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL)
- Authentication: Supabase Auth

### Vision
Our vision is to build a reliable and accessible digital blood donation network, starting in Pakistan, that reduces delays in finding blood donors and supports faster emergency response.
